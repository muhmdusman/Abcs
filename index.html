About Us

A Student-Led Cloud Community at NUST

AWS Student Builder Group NUST is a student-led, student-driven community helping students learn cloud computing, build real projects, and grow their careers on AWS. We make the cloud approachable for everyone, from absolute beginners to aspiring architects.

  • Hands-on workshops, study jams, and labs
  • Mentorship, certification prep, and career guidance
  • A welcoming community to network and build together

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AWS Student Builder Group NUST?
We are a student-led, student-driven cloud community at NUST, part of the AWS Student Builder Group program. We help students learn cloud computing and AI, build real projects, and grow their careers on AWS through workshops, study jams, and community events.
Who can join? Do I need prior experience?
Any NUST student can join, no prior cloud or coding experience required. Whether you're a complete beginner or already building, you're welcome. We have something for everyone.
How do I become a member?
It's free! Just reach out through any of our social channels or the contact page, and join our WhatsApp community to get updates on upcoming sessions and events.
Is there any membership fee?
No. Joining the community and attending our sessions and events is completely free.
What kind of events do you organize?
Hands-on workshops, certification study jams, tech talks with industry speakers, community days, and hackathons, all focused on cloud, AI, and building real skills.
Will I get a certificate or AWS swag?
Yes! Participants can earn certificates of participation, and we regularly share AWS swag and goodies for active members and event participants.
